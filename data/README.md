# Data

The data are not in this repository. They are the authors' survey files from the Management Science replication package of Paaso, Pursiainen and Torstila (2025), available on the paper's page: https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2022.03007

To rebuild the folder, download the replication package, unzip it, and copy these two files from its `Data/` folder into `data/raw/paaso/`:

- `cleaned_survey.dta`: survey of Finnish owner-managers, May to June 2020
- `2023_cleaned_survey.dta`: follow-up survey, April 2023

Under the journal's terms the files may be used to verify the published results; other uses need the authors' permission. Do not share or post them.
